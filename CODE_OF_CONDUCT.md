# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Participants should be respectful, patient, technically honest, and careful
with device safety and privacy. Helpful behavior includes documenting evidence,
acknowledging uncertainty, accepting failed or partial field reports, and giving
constructive review feedback.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized
language, deliberate disclosure of another person's private information,
publishing device credentials or unique radio data, and pressuring contributors
to claim tests or adoption that did not occur.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository Issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project spaces. Public concerns can be raised in an Issue. Confidential conduct
or safety concerns can be sent through GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting
channel and should be marked as a conduct report.

The core maintainer may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or
ban a participant when necessary. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate,
documented when safe, and protect reporters' privacy.
